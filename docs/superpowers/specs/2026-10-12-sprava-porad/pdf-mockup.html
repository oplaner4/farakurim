A. Day blocks

Each day is a band: date, weekday and feast on the left, its services on the right; every row names its place (no merged cells). Sundays and solemnities get a grey band and capitals. Calm and very readable.

B. Refreshed table

Closest to today's DOCX: the same five columns, without merged cells or the outer grid; thin rules, a heavier rule between days, grey Sundays. Familiar for the parish.

Both: announcements at 10.5 pt equivalent for reading aloud, intentions in italics, Sundays/solemnities bold + grey (works in black-and-white), hidden-from-web intentions still printed. If the week doesn't fit one page, mPDF first tightens the schedule, then the announcements continue on page 2 (never shrinking the read-aloud text below 10 pt).
